01 — The house

A green garden, a shaded veranda, and the sunset over the sea.

Welcome to Ilios Residence, a bright and spacious private home in the peaceful Panorama area of Paleochora, Chania. The house has two comfortable bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, a cosy living room, one bathroom and a separate WC.

Relax in the lush private garden or eat on the furnished veranda while the sun goes down over the sea. It is a welcoming base for couples, families or friends who want to enjoy Paleochora in comfort and privacy — with free parking right on the property.

04 — Location

Paleochora, on the south-west coast of Crete.

The house is in Panorama, the quiet residential area just above the village. Paleochora has a sandy beach on one side of the peninsula and a pebble one on the other, plus a working harbour, a market and enough tavernas to eat somewhere different every night.

05 — Nearby

Beaches, gorges and long dinners.

06 — Getting here

From the airport to the front gate.

  1. 01

    Fly to Chania (CHQ)

    Heraklion (HER) also works, around three hours away. Direct flights run from most of Europe in season.

  2. 02

    Car, taxi or bus

    A hire car is easiest. Private transfers can be arranged on request, and KTEL buses run daily from Chania.

  3. 03

    Arrive at the house

    Park for free on the property. Self check-in from 15:00 with a lockbox — directions and the code are sent a few days before you arrive.

How do I book the house? +

Reservations are handled through Airbnb, so payment and cancellation follow their terms. Send an enquiry here if you have a question first.

Do we need a car? +

Not for the village itself, everything is walkable. A car helps for the beaches and gorges further along the coast.

Is the house suitable for young children? +

Yes. There is a travel cot in the house, a high chair on request, and the garden is private and set back from the road.

When is the best time to visit? +

May, June, September and October are warm and quiet. July and August are busier and hotter, with the sea at its warmest.

Is linen and cleaning included? +

Yes. Linen, towels and the basics — soap, shampoo, toilet paper — are provided, and the house is cleaned before every arrival.
